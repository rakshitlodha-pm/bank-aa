Family A · always fires

Emergency fund

This one speaks in every situation. Short of cover it says how to close the gap; at cover it says well done and points the rest at investing. The situation changes the tone and where the money is sent — never whether we speak.

Rule

Fires when balance ÷ median month < 1. Suggests a starting amount rather than the target: round(median × 2.5%) to the nearest ₹500.

Never states the shortfall as a lump at this level — ₹12.2L short reads as impossible and is the most common reason people close this card and never return.

The target is deliberately not the headline. A user four days from zero cannot act on ₹12,39,554, so the number that gets the button is the one they can start tomorrow.

Family B · the sharpest one in the dump

Investing that slipped

The reference user ran ₹2,05,000 a month for eight months, missed May and June, then came back at ₹1,45,000 and stayed there. The nudge is not the two missed months — it is that the level dropped by ₹60,000 and never recovered.

Rule

Fires when the median monthly investment of the last 3 months is below the median of the 9 before it by more than 15%.

The figure quoted is Σ (old level − actual) across every month since the drop, so a pause and a step-down are counted as one number.

The dismissal is a real option, not a delay — some step-downs are deliberate, and the engine cannot tell.

A missed-SIP alert would have read "two months missed" and been wrong. The pause was ₹3,90,000; the quiet step-down since July is another ₹1,19,899 and is still running.

The second button is not "Later." A deliberate reduction — a job change, a new EMI — should be accepted and stop the nudge, not postponed into next month.

Family C · no budget, no target

Expenses running hot

Nothing here is measured against a number the user chose. It is measured against their own median month, and it names the cause where we honestly know it — which on this dump is a billing-cycle artefact rather than behaviour.

Family E · the rule working

Counted, never nudged

Health, gambling, penalties, welfare and insurance are counted in every total and never earn a nudge, a celebration or a button. The frame below is what that looks like — which is to say, almost nothing.

Rule

Still yours to call. If the blocklist goes, this becomes an ordinary category with a nudge and a button. Nothing else on this page changes either way.

What these fourteen frames settle

Decided

  • No budget to set. The engine nudges; the user configures nothing
  • Everything is measured against the user's own median month — never against last month, never against a target they picked
  • The emergency fund always speaks, including when it is finished
  • Surplus size picks the destination, not whether we talk: under 3× a month stays liquid, over 3× goes long
  • Nudges may have no ask. Three of the fourteen carry no button
  • No verdict before day eight of a period; composition instead
  • Every nudge names its cause when the engine honestly knows it

Open

  • The sensitive blocklist — E1 is drawn both ways and is the only frame that changes
  • Does the frequency nudge (D2) ever get a tone, or stay a bare count
  • How many nudges render at once before the rest go behind an expander
  • Whether a hot month suppresses buy buttons elsewhere on the page in the same session

Found in the dump while drawing these

  • The mandate stepped down, not just paused. ₹2,05,000 → ₹1,45,000, ₹5,09,899 behind the old rate
  • Two card statements landed in August — ₹1,05,164 and ₹21,656. Most of the 1.5× month
  • Astrotalk took 74 payments averaging ₹250 — the highest-frequency merchant in the year
  • Twelve recurring services, ₹41,294 — none with a listed Indian parent
  • The median month depends hard on the window: ₹2,06,592 over six months, ₹1,21,924 over twelve. Spending stepped up mid-year, so the shorter window is the honest one

Source. One Account Aggregator deposits dump — 1,265 transactions across three savings accounts, 2 August 2025 to 1 September 2026. Frames marked Real are computed from it; frames marked Fixture use invented figures to reach a situation this user never hits. Personal identifiers in the file were read and excluded.

Caution. One user is one user. Every threshold on this page — the 6× cover target, the 3× surplus split, the 1.25× hot-month trigger, the 40-payment frequency floor, the day-8 quiet period — is a first guess that needs tuning against real traffic.
