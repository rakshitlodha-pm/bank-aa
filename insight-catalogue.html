The ranking worksheet

36

My proposal in one column, yours blank in the next.

Thirty-six components is far more than any page should render, which is exactly why the queue matters. But the first cut is not a priority at all — it is structure against slots. 11 of these always render: the money tiles, the balances, the category ranking, the partial-month handling, the states. They are the page, not candidates for it. That leaves 25 ranked slots, and my split is 8 at P0 plus one P0 alternate, 9 at P1 and 7 at P2. The alternate is the interesting one: category → sector only earns P0 when merchant → parent fails to fire, so its priority depends on the data rather than being fixed. If each section renders three slots, a user as rich as you never sees past P0 — so P1 exists almost entirely for thinner data, and that tier is where I would expect you to disagree with me.
